## Hi, I'm OoJae

I'm a final-year Geology student, a crypto writer at MEXC, and the founder of [RemitRoute](https://github.com/OoJae/remitroute): an always-on agent on Celo that runs recurring stablecoin savings, FX and remittances through MiniPay. You set one rule and your money runs itself. [Live](https://remitroute.vercel.app)

Beyond that, I build where AI agents and money meet: agents that can pay and get paid, escrow that only releases when the work is proven, and privacy tools for identity and finance. I ship end to end: contracts, backend and front end.

I've shipped on Base, Solana, Celo, Midnight, Starknet, Aleo, Sui, Flare, X Layer, Robinhood Chain and more.
